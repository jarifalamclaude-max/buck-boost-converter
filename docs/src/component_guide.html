SafeBuck-80 — Kon component, keno

80 W protected 4-switch buck-boost · 9–36 V in · 1.6–36 V / 0.9–8.3 A out (CV + CC) · 72 × 54 mm · 2-layer 2 oz

1. Puro circuit ta ek nojore

VIN (J1) → F1 fuse → D1 TVS → LM74930 + Q1/Q2 (ulta polarity block, over/under-voltage cut-off) → LT8390 + Q3–Q6 + L1 (4-switch synchronous buck-boost: input er cheye output beshi ba kom dutoi) → R31 (CC current sense) → F2 → VOUT+ (J2). VOUT− er return path e Q7 (low-side switch) ache. Output e keu battery ulta lagale Q7 1–6 µs er moddhe off hoy ar red OUT LED jole. Input ulta dile red IN LED jole, kichu gorom hoy na.

Keno 4-switch buck-boost? QS-1212CCBD te UC3843 (non-synchronous, diode diye) — diode e 0.5 V drop, 80 W e onek gorom. LT8390 te 4-ta MOSFET synchronous switch kore, tai efficiency 93–97 % (hisab nicher table e), ar input 9 V hok ba 36 V, output jekono voltage (1.6–36 V) smooth bhabe dey — buck, boost ar buck-boost mode nije nije bodlay.

2. Main IC

U1 — LT8390 (Analog Devices) · LCSC C673802

  • 60 V, 4-switch synchronous buck-boost controller, peak-current-mode (buck e peak-buck, boost e peak-boost) — stable ar fast.
  • CV + CC dutoi ek IC te: FB pin (1.00 V) diye voltage, CTRL pin diye output current limit (RV2 pot). Eta-i "CC/CV module" er kaj.
  • ±15 % spread spectrum (SYNC/SPRD → INTVCC): EMI peak kome.
  • ISP/ISN e 0.75 V hole (62.5 A) sathe sathe switching bondho — output short e MOSFET bache. SS → VREF 100 k = "keep-running" mode: short hole CC te current limit kore chalte thake, latch kore na.
  • Abs max 60 V: tai input e ~43 V er upore LM74930 cut-off kore dey.
  • FB pin abs max 6 V: R46 (10 k) + D14 (5.1 V zener) pin take rokkha kore — output e CV er cheye onek beshi voltage er battery lagaleo.

U2 — LM74930-Q1 (Texas Instruments) · LCSC C22428070

  • Ideal-diode + load-switch controller, −65 V porjonto ulta voltage sojjo kore. Q2 (DGATE) ulta polarity block kore, Q1 (HGATE) over-voltage/under-voltage e line kete dey.
  • UV/OV ladder (R5 280k, R6 17.8k, R7 4.22k): UV 7.5 V (falling) / 8.2 V (rising), OV 42.9 V cut-off, 39.4 V e abar on.
  • CdVdT (C5 3.3 nF + R4 100 Ω) inrush ke ~1.3–2.6 A te rakhe — hot-plug e spark/fuse problem nai.
  • D17 (15 V zener, TI er DZ1): chalu obosthay input short hole C5 Q1 er gate ke upore dhore rakhe — zener VGS 15 V er niche rakhe (Q1 limit 20 V).
  • FLT pin → red IN LED. Built-in circuit breaker disable kora (TMR, ILIM → GND; CS+/CS−/ISCP → C): F1 fuse shudhu MOSFET short hole last resort.
  • MODE → VS, OVCLAMP → GND, NC pin float, exposed pad (RTN) float — datasheet onujayi.

3. Power parts

Q3–Q6 — CSD18532Q5B ×4 (TI, 60 V) · LCSC C882766

LT8390 er gate drive 5 V (INTVCC), tai logic-level MOSFET lagbe: 4.5 V e 4.3 mΩ max. Gate charge kom (~19 nC @4.5 V) — LT8390 er INTVCC regulator e loss kom (~0.6–1 W @36 V). 60 V rating, OV cut-off (~43 V, worst 46 V) er upore margin. SON 5×6 package, PCB te heat chhore dey. (CSD18540Q5B ar o bhalo, kintu JLCPCB te stock e 2-ta — tai eta.)

Q1, Q2, Q7 — CSD19532Q5B ×3 (TI, 100 V) · LCSC C473333

Input protection (Q1/Q2) ar output reverse-battery switch (Q7). Q7 ke output voltage + ulta battery = ~70 V porjonto block korte hoy, tai 100 V. 4.9 mΩ (@10 V) — 8 A te mattro ~0.3 W. Avalanche rating 274 mJ: fault e wiring inductance er energy (hisab: 0.6–19 mJ) easily sojjo kore.

D15 — PMEG10030ELP (Nexperia 100 V 3 A Schottky) · LCSC C5260346

GND theke VOUT_C (anode GND). Motor/solenoid er moto inductive load hole converter bondho howar muhurte coil er current eta diye ghore — VOUT_C ~−1 V e thake, tai Q11 false trip kore na ar Q7 e avalanche hoy na. Ulta battery hit eo VOUT_C ke ~−1.5 V er niche jete dey na (LT8390 er VOUT/ISP/ISN pin bache). Low-leakage (450 nA @100 V) bole normal e loss nai.

L1 — Coilcraft XAL1510-103 (10 µH) · LCSC C3911782

10 µH keno: slope-compensation er jonno L > 10·VOUT·RSENSE/f = 3.6 µH lagbe, ar ripple current ~30–40 % e rakhte 10 µH thik. Isat 26.3 A (soft saturation) > peak current limit max 21.7 A. DCR 9 mΩ max (kom loss), shielded (EMI kom), AEC-Q200.

R15 3 mΩ (RSENSE) · R31 12 mΩ (RIS)

R15: LT8390 er inductor peak-current sense — limit 11.7–21.7 A (datasheet threshold / 3 mΩ). R31: output current sense — CC full scale 100 mV / 12 mΩ = 8.33 A. Duto-i Kelvin connection (net-tie NT1–NT4) diye — power current sense line diye jay na, tai accurate.

Capacitors

  • C7, C8 (2×10 µF 100 V X7S 1210): hot loop 1 er input cap, MOSFET er thik niche. 100 V rating keno: ceramic e DC-bias e capacitance kome — 36 V e 100 V cap ~45 % thake, 50 V cap hole ~20 % thakto.
  • C11 (100 µF 63 V electrolytic): ESR ~0.35 Ω — input cable er inductance + ceramic cap er LC resonance damp kore (Middlebrook). Hot-plug e voltage spike kome.
  • C12–C14, C36 (4×10 µF 100 V) + C18 (47 µF 50 V hybrid polymer, Panasonic ZA): output ripple ar load step. 300 kHz e ceramic er reactance kom na — tai hybrid o ripple current bhag kore (~2.2 A worst corner e, 85 °C e rating ~2.5 A).
  • C19/C20/C21/C22/C23: IC pin er bypass — protitar pin er 2–5 mm er moddhe.

F1 15 A, F2 10 A (Littelfuse 451 NANO²)

Normal fault (ulta, short, over-voltage) e fuse kete na — electronics-i protect kore. Fuse shudhu kono MOSFET nijei short hoye gele aagun thekate.

D1 — SMBJ60CA (bidirectional TVS)

Bidirectional bole −60 V ulta input eo conduct kore na (LM74930 block korbe), kintu cable er fast spike (hot-plug, inductive kick) clamp kore. 60 V standoff, clamp 96.8 V (< 100 V of Q1/Q2).
