1. Board ta ek nojore ki kore

SafeBuck-80 ekta automatic buck-boost converter: input e 9–36 V jai dao, output e tumi je voltage set korbe (1.6–36 V) seta-i debe — input er cheye kom hok (buck) ba beshi (boost). Sathe current er limit (CC, 0.94–8.33 A) set kora jay — battery charge ba LED chalanor jonno. Sorbochcho 80 W.

Duita knob
RV1 = CV (koto volt) · RV2 = CC (sorbochcho koto amp). Duitai 25-turn trimpot — onek bar ghurate hoy, tai sukkho bhabe set kora jay.
Tinta LED
Sobuj ON = controller chalu · Lal IN = input ulta / beshi / kom volt · Lal OUT = output e battery ulta.
"Smoke-tester" er dorshon: jekono vul connection (input ulta, output e battery ulta, output short, beshi voltage, beshi gorom) e board pure jabe na — shudhu ekta lal LED jolbe ba current kome jabe. Fuse (F1, F2) shudhu sesh rokkha — normal vul e kate na.

3. Buck kivabe kaj kore — dhape dhape

3.1 Somossa: 24 V theke 12 V lagbe, 6.67 A (80 W)

Resistor diye komale (linear)
Bakhi 12 V resistor e "khoy" hoy: 12 V × 6.67 A = 80 W gorom — ekta heater! Efficiency 50 %.
Buck diye
Loss mattro 3.93 W — efficiency 95.3–96.1 %. Input theke lage mattro 3.5 A (output 6.67 A er-o kom!).

3.2 Mul buddhi: druto on-off kore "gor" (average) neya

Switch ta jodi somoy er 50 % on ar 50 % off thake, tahole gor voltage = 24 V er 50 % = 12 V. 25 % on rakhle 6 V, 75 % e 18 V. On thakar ongsho ke bole duty cycle (D).

Buck: Vout = D × Vin   (24 V × 0.5 = 12 V)

Kintu kacha on-off (24 V, 0 V, 24 V…) load e dile load jhatka khabe. Tai majhe L1 (flywheel) ar C (tank) boshano hoy — era jhatka ke mosrin DC te bodle dey. Ekhon dekhi ek cycle e ki ghote:

Dhap 1 — A on, B off

Input → A → L1 → load → ferot. L1 er dui pashe 24 − 12 = 12 V, tai current barte thake (12 V / 10 µH = 1.2 A protiti µs e). L1 shakti jomay.

Dhap 2 — A off, B on

Input bichchinno. Kintu L1 current hothat thamate dey na — B diye GND theke current tene load e pathay. Ekhon L1 er upor −12 V, tai current dhire komte thake.

3.3 Current er chobi (24 V → 12 V, 6.67 A)

Protiti cycle 3.33 µs: 1.67 µs A on (current 5.67 → 7.67 A), 1.67 µs B on (7.67 → 5.67 A). Load er kache gor 6.67 A, capacitor baki chhoto dheu (ripple) shushe ney — output e ripple mattro ~21 mV. Eta 1 second e 300,000 bar hoy.

Keno B te diode na, MOSFET? (synchronous) Purono design (jemon QS-1212 er UC3843) e B er jaygay diode thake: 0.5 V × 6.67 A ≈ 3.3 W gorom. MOSFET (4.3 mΩ) e oi kaje loss mattro ~0.1 W. Tai efficiency 95 %+.
A ar B kokhono eksathe on na! Hole input sorasori GND e short (shoot-through). U1 majhe khub chhoto "dead time" rakhe — ekta puro off hole tarpor onyota on.

4. Boost ar 4-switch buck-boost

4.1 Boost: 12 V theke 24 V (input er cheye beshi)

Dhap 1 — C on, D off

L1 sorasori input theke GND e — current bare, shakti jome. Load ke ei somoy capacitor chalay (nil).

Dhap 2 — C off, D on

L1 current thamate dey na, nijer voltage input er sathe jog kore output e dhele dey → output input er cheye beshi.

Boost: Vout = Vin / (1 − D)   (12 V / (1 − 0.5) = 24 V)

5. Brain: U1 (LT8390) kivabe thik kore

Gari-r cruise control er moto: speed (voltage) mepe accelerator (duty) kom-beshi kore. U1 tin jaygay chokh rakhe:

  • CV (voltage): RV1 + R25 output er ekta tukro FB pin e dey. U1 sobsomoy FB = 1.00 V rakhte chay. Output kome gele duty barai, beshi hole komay.
  • CC (current): R31 (12 mΩ) e output current er voltage. RV2 je limit set kore, current tar beshi hote chaile U1 voltage namiye dey.
  • Peak limit (protiti cycle): R15 (3 mΩ) diye L1 er current. 11.7–21.7 A er beshi hote dey na — short e MOSFET bache. Ei "peak-current-mode" control loop ke stable ar druto kore.
Battery charge udahoron: CV 14.4 V, CC 5 A set korle — khali battery te prothome current 5 A e atke thake (CC), voltage dhire bare. 14.4 V e pouchale CV shuru, current nije nije kome. Je limit aage pouchay, sei-i jete.

9. Design ta kivabe banalam

  1. Requirement: QS-1212CCBD er moto 80 W auto buck-boost — 9–36 V in, 1.6–36 V out, CV + CC, ar "smoke-tester" protection: vul connection e kichu pure na, shudhu lal LED.
  2. Topology: 4-switch synchronous buck-boost. QS-1212 er UC3843 + diode design e diode onek gorom hoy. LT8390 nilam: 60 V, CV + CC ek IC te, 4 ta MOSFET chalay, spread spectrum, JLC te pawa jay.
  3. Protection architecture: input e LM74930 + duita mukhomukhi MOSFET (ulta, OV, UV, inrush); output e low-side Q7 + transistor trip circuit (ulta battery); FB/ISP-ISN clamp; PTC thermal foldback; fuse shudhu sesh rokkha.
  4. Part bachai: protiti part er LCSC number (JLC assembly). Ceramic capacitor rating ≥ 2 × voltage, MOSFET 60/100 V margin, inductor saturation > current limit max.
  5. Hisab (calc/design.py): current limit, inductor, ripple, loss, temperature, sob threshold, ar ulta-battery hot-plug er numeric simulation — 57 ta check, 56 PASS (1 ta info: heatsink charai +46 °C).
  6. Ek netlist theke sob: hardware/netlist.py → schematic (make_schematic.py) → PCB (build_pcb.py + placement.py) → routing (Freerouting + nijer completion router route.py) → verify.py (schematic = netlist = PCB).
  7. Layout rule: hot loop (C7/C8 ↔ Q3/Q4, C12–C36 ↔ Q5/Q6) chhoto; Kelvin sense net-tie; 2 oz copper; GND pour + stitching via; gate track chhoto (TG2 97 → 44 mm).
  8. Check: KiCad DRC — 0 unconnected, 0 clearance error. Independent review er poramorsho fix.
  9. Board 72 × 54 mm: gorom komate ektu boro (+57 → +46 °C), terminal er mukh baire.
  10. Fabrication: make_jlcpcb.py → gerber, BOM, CPL (JLCPCB), KiCad project kicad/ folder e.
Sotti kotha: board ta ekhono banano/test kora hoyni — sob hisab ar software check. Prothom bar chalu korar somoy Build Guide er dhap (current-limited supply, kom voltage diye shuru) mene cholo. Compensation (R22/C29) load-step test e thik korte hote pare.
